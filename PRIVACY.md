# Privacy notice

The authoritative copy of this notice is the one inside the application (version 3.10.2). This file reproduces it so it can be read on GitHub without opening the app.

---

PRIVACY NOTICE

App: Rudrabha Mukherjee's Design Helper, version 3.10.2
Created by Rudrabha Mukherjee

SHORT VERSION
This app collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign in and
no tracking. Your work never leaves your device unless you choose to send
it somewhere yourself.

WHAT IS COLLECTED
Nothing. No name, no email, no phone number, no location, no usage figures,
no error reports, no advertising identifiers.

WHERE YOUR WORK IS KEPT
Inside your own browser, on this device only. Nobody else can read it,
including the person who made this app, and including whoever hosts the
address you opened it from. It is never uploaded.

NETWORK
Once it is open, this app sends none of your work anywhere, and fetches
nothing to display itself. The one exception is the version check: when
you open the app, it asks the address it came from whether a newer version
exists, and your browser separately makes the same kind of check on its
own. That request carries no information about you or your work beyond what
any web page request carries — your internet address and the time, which
the company hosting the address records. Nothing you have written is ever
part of it. You can switch the check off in Settings, and if you saved the
app as a file and opened the file, no check happens at all.

HOW YOU GOT THIS APP
If somebody gave you this app as a file, nothing about you reached anybody
at any point.

If instead you opened it as a web page at an address, then your device
asked that website for the page, exactly as it would for any website. The
company hosting that address will have recorded your internet address and
the time, as nearly all websites do, and their privacy rules cover that
record, not this notice. That happens once, when the page loads. It does
not send any of your work, because your work is not sent anywhere at all.
If you would rather nobody saw even that, save the page as a file and open
the file instead.

COOKIES AND TRACKING
None. There are no cookies, no pixels, no analytics and no advertising.

WHEN YOUR WORK DOES LEAVE THIS DEVICE
Only when you press a button to make that happen:
- Copy puts words on your clipboard.
- Share hands the words to your own phone's share box, so you can choose
  where they go.
- Save as a file puts a file in your downloads folder.
When you then paste those words into an AI chat, that AI company's own
privacy rules apply, not this app's. Read theirs before sending anything
private.

LOCK
If you turn on the lock, your work is scrambled on this device using a key
made from your password. Nobody, including the maker of this app, can
unscramble it without that password.

CHILDREN
This app collects nothing, so it collects nothing about children either.

DELETING YOUR WORK
Open Settings and press "Empty this app and start again". Clearing your
browser data also removes it. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

CHANGES
This notice describes this version of the app. A file copy you already have
on your own device will keep behaving as that older copy did until you open
a newer file.
